SYNTAX:
----------------

 FROM table_name  
 [WHERE <filter_condition>]
 GROUP BY column_name/expression ;

--------------------------------------
1-FROM 
2-WHERE(if used)  [ROW-BY-ROW]
3-GROUP BY        [ROW-BY-ROW]
4-SELECT          [GROUP-BY-GROUP]
