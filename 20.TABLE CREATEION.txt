TABLE CREATION PROJECTS

1. E-COMMERCE MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

Tables:

ECOMMERCE_CUSTOMERS
customer_id, first_name, last_name, email, phone, address, created_at

ECOMMERCE_CATEGORIES
category_id, category_name, description

ECOMMERCE_PRODUCTS
product_id, product_name, description, price, stock_quantity, category_id

ECOMMERCE_ORDERS
order_id, customer_id, order_date, total_amount, order_status

ECOMMERCE_ORDER_ITEMS
order_item_id, order_id, product_id, quantity, unit_price

ECOMMERCE_PAYMENTS
payment_id, order_id, payment_date, payment_method, payment_amount, payment_status

Business Rules:
1. Customer email and phone must be unique.
2. Category name must be unique.
3. Product price must be greater than zero.
4. Stock quantity cannot be negative.
5. Every product must belong to a category.
6. Every order must belong to a customer.
7. Quantity must be greater than zero.
8. Payment status must be PENDING, SUCCESS, FAILED, or REFUNDED.


2. EMPLOYEE AND PAYROLL MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

Tables:

EMPLOYEE_DEPARTMENTS
department_id, department_name, location

EMPLOYEE_EMPLOYEES
employee_id, first_name, last_name, email, phone, designation, salary, hire_date, department_id, manager_id

EMPLOYEE_ATTENDANCE
attendance_id, employee_id, attendance_date, attendance_status, check_in_time, check_out_time

EMPLOYEE_PAYROLL
payroll_id, employee_id, pay_month, basic_salary, bonus, deductions, net_salary

EMPLOYEE_LEAVE_REQUESTS
leave_id, employee_id, leave_type, from_date, to_date, leave_status

Business Rules:
1. Department name must be unique.
2. Employee email and phone must be unique.
3. Salary must be greater than zero.
4. Every employee must belong to a valid department.
5. manager_id references another employee.
6. Attendance status must be PRESENT, ABSENT, or LEAVE.
7. Bonus and deductions cannot be negative.
8. Leave end date must not be earlier than the start date.

5. LIBRARY MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

Tables:

LIBRARY_AUTHORS
author_id, author_name, country

LIBRARY_BOOKS
book_id, title, isbn, category, publisher, publication_year

LIBRARY_BOOK_AUTHORS
book_id, author_id

LIBRARY_BOOK_COPIES
copy_id, book_id, copy_number, copy_status

LIBRARY_MEMBERS
member_id, member_name, email, phone, join_date

LIBRARY_BORROW_RECORDS
borrow_id, copy_id, member_id, issue_date, due_date, return_date

Business Rules:
1. ISBN must be unique.
2. Member email and phone must be unique.
3. Each book copy must belong to a valid book.
4. Every borrow record must reference a valid member and book copy.
5. Due date must not be earlier than issue date.
6. Copy status must be AVAILABLE, BORROWED, or DAMAGED.
7. A book can have multiple authors, and an author can write multiple books.

HOTEL_ROOMS

HOTEL_GUESTS

HOTEL_RESERVATIONS
reservation_id, guest_id, room_id, check_in_date, check_out_date, booking_status

HOTEL_PAYMENTS
payment_id, reservation_id, payment_date, amount, payment_method, payment_status

HOTEL_HOTEL_SERVICES

2. Room capacity must be greater than zero.

LEARNING_STUDENTS

LEARNING_COURSES
course_id, course_name, description, duration, course_fee, instructor_id

LEARNING_ENROLLMENTS
enrollment_id, student_id, course_id, enrollment_date, enrollment_status

LEARNING_ASSIGNMENTS

submission_id, assignment_id, student_id, submitted_at, marks_obtained, submission_status

LEARNING_CERTIFICATES

6. Certificate number must be unique.
7. Enrollment status must be ACTIVE, COMPLETED, or CANCELLED.

6. Every application must reference a valid applicant and job.
7. Experience years cannot be negative.
