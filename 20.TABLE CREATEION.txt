1. Student Management System

---

Scenario

A school wants to maintain student details and the marks obtained by students in different subjects.

Tables

Students
student_id, first_name, last_name, gender, date_of_birth, email, phone, address, admission_date

Subjects
subject_id, subject_name, subject_code

Marks
mark_id, student_id, subject_id, marks, exam_type, exam_date

Business Rules

* Every student must have a unique student ID.
* Email and phone number should be unique.
* Gender should be Male, Female, or Other.
* Marks should be between 0 and 100.
* Every mark must belong to one student.
* Every mark must belong to one subject.
* Admission date should default to the current date.
